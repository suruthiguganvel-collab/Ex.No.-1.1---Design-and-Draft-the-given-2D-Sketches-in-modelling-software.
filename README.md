# Ex.No.-1.1-Design and Draft the given 2D Sketches in modelling software.

## AIM
To create and draft the given 2D engineering sketch using Autodesk Fusion by applying sketching tools, geometric constraints, dimensions, mirror and trim operations.

## REQUIREMENTS
1. Autodesk fusion 360.
2. Windows 10.
3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 2 GB or more.)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download;500 Kbps or faster upload.

## PROCEDURE
1. Launch Autodesk Fusion, start a fresh design file, click Create Sketch under the Sketch panel, and pick the Front plane to draw on.

2.Inside the sketch workspace, grab the Line tool from the Sketch toolbar. Sketch the base horizontal reference layout per the reference drawing and assign the needed lengths using Sketch Dimension.

<img width="912" height="652" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fd55cd62-7b60-4ee7-9fbb-0208b21a42d5" />

3. Set up the primary axis/reference lines and mark key center points from the drawing specifications, keeping a 47 mm horizontal spacing between the designated centers and applying the vertical offsets as shown.

4. Activate the Circle tool to place the main round features at their given coordinates, setting the diameters to Ø20, Ø32, Ø10, and Ø20 according to the drawing.

5. Build the top circular boss by sketching the required concentric circles, entering their size values, and aligning them through their shared center points.
 
6. Shape the outer contour using Line and Arc tools, applying the specified curve radii including R64 and R20.

7. Add geometric constraints (such as tangency/coincidence) along with dimensions so all arcs and lines tie cleanly into the existing circles and reference lines, preserving the exact part geometry.

<img width="742" height="436" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5e12ae7d-6039-4db8-8cd6-04c595ffba2b" />
<img width="712" height="417" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c6055e7d-d5a1-48df-bacc-dfb2b60eba69" />

8. Draw the two lateral circular features with the Circle tool, place them at their given horizontal and vertical positions, and set their radii including R7 and R10.

9. Add the lower central round feature along the bottom axis by drawing the required circles and specifying diameters of Ø20 and Ø10.

10. Use the Arc tool to blend the lower circular elements into the main outer boundary, assigning the required radii so every transition flows smoothly.
11. Select Mirror to duplicate the symmetric features across the appropriate centerline/reference axis by choosing the entities to copy and picking the axis line as the mirror plane.
<img width="802" height="556" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/30f23f6c-f7af-4ddb-87a5-66124bf2e612" />
 
12. Inspect both sides of the mirrored geometry to verify symmetry and accurate placement.  
13. Create the central repeating/patterned feature using circles and arcs—draw the center circle first, then form the surrounding repeated curved segments per the drawing.
14. Dimension the central feature—including Ø40, Ø66, and R6—and apply the corresponding pattern or mirror operation to generate the repeated shape.  
15. Use Trim to clean up extra arc segments, circle overlaps, and stray line extensions that do not belong to the final profile.

<img width="657" height="320" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d68feb9a-4746-4b70-99a5-f5fc3920a133" />

16. Keep trimming unneeded segments until the exterior contour and interior cutouts match the reference drawing, with no leftover construction overlap.
17. Double-check key dimensions and positions across the finished sketch, including the 65 mm vertical span, 47 mm horizontal span, 26 mm and 24 mm vertical dimensions, and all called-out diameters and radii.  
18. Fix any discrepancies and confirm the completed sketch includes the top circular boss, side holes, lower circular feature, outer curved contour, and central patterned feature.  
19. Fit/zoom out so the entire 2D sketch and its dimension callouts are clearly visible, then capture a screenshot for your report.
